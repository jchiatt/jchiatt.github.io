01 · Deck identity

Value is hidden in the Tribute.

The build does not chase the largest possible board. It establishes a Ritual boss, banks reactive cards, and makes the opponent trade into Tribute effects that replace themselves.

儀

Exact-level Ritual engine

Mitsurugi Ritual turns two Level 4 Reptiles in the Deck into a Level 8 boss plus multiple Tribute triggers.

鎖

Interaction across zones

Impulse, Fydraulis, Super Polymerization, Fallen and set Mitsurugi cards attack from different locations.

還

Recursive bosses

Murakumo and Futsu convert interruption into new cards, revival and another wave of pressure.

剣
The goal is not to remain untouched.

The goal is for every opposing answer to trigger another Mitsurugi resource.

AramasaThe Normal Summon that starts everything.
Mitsurugi RitualTwo Level 4s become a Level 8 and two triggers.
Mitsurugi PrayersTribute conversion at Quick-Play speed.
WousuBoss removal—after every non-Reptile action.

03 · Turn plan

Choose the posture before the line.

Going first, this is layered Ritual control. Going second, it waits for the opponent to expose the targets that its utility package can convert.

04 · Line library

Repeatable lines, not fantasy hands.

Compact templates designed to branch around interruption and the deck’s lingering restrictions.

Ritual + Level 8 boss → two Level 4 triggers
  1. Activate Mitsurugi Ritual to Ritual Summon the Level 8 Reptile from hand.
  2. Use Aramasa + Saji from the Deck when both are available.
  3. Build the Tribute-trigger Chain so the most important search is insulated.
  4. Going first, favor Great Purification or Prayers; going second, favor removal and extension.
Output: a Ritual boss, a Mitsurugi monster search, and a Mitsurugi Spell/Trap search.
Habakiri access → Saji conversion
  1. Reveal Habakiri and summon Saji from the Deck.
  2. Tribute Saji to search the missing Mitsurugi Spell/Trap.
  3. Use Ritual or Prayers to convert the hand into a properly summoned boss.
  4. Do not burn Habakiri’s once-per-Duel effect on a line that does not improve the final interaction map.
Lamia → Magnamhut protection line
  1. Use Lamia as Habakiri access or Ritual material, placing it in the GY.
  2. Special Summon Magnamhut by banishing Lamia when a legal LIGHT/DARK target exists.
  3. Lamia protects a Reptile boss from effect destruction through the end of the next turn.
Doctrine: free upside from a good card—not a line worth weakening the deck to force.
Fydraulis full reveal → Synchro send
  1. Keep all five Synchros in the Extra Deck before the first full Fydraulis resolution.
  2. Reveal the bank, Special Summon Fydraulis and send the Synchro whose GY effect best answers the board.
  3. Malong is the bounce; Packbit preserves WATER functionality after Impulse.
  4. Resolve activated non-Synchro Extra Deck effects first.
Fallen + Albion → removal into follow-up
  1. Activate The Fallen & The Virtuous, send Albion, and destroy a face-up card.
  2. In the End Phase, Albion can Set another Fallen because it is always treated as a Branded card.
  3. Use The Dragon that Devours the Dogma when direct follow-up is more valuable.
Dyna Mondo recovery pivot
  1. Make Dyna Mondo after the Ritual bodies have already paid for themselves.
  2. Use its interaction to reset a field card and a Ritual in the GY.
  3. On the opponent’s turn, Tribute Dyna Mondo to revive a properly summoned Murakumo.
  4. Murakumo’s summon threatens the wipe and another search.

07 · Matchups & siding

Side by role, not reflex.

Exact cuts change with turn order and the opponent’s list. These maps are starting points rather than rigid scripts.

Extra Deck combo

Kewl Tune / tuner-heavy boards

Keep Super Polymerization and Theseus live. Fuwalos and Sloth punish field size; Droplet compresses established interaction.

Bring in3 Fuwalos · 2 Droplet · 3 Pot of Sloth
Common cutsPurification going second · Magnamhut when targets are sparse · Fallen flex slots
Mitsurugi mirror

Do not Tribute their bosses casually.

Super Polymerization is premium because Fusion Material is not Tributed. Wousu can trigger an opposing Ritual boss, so it is not automatic removal.

Bring inDroplet · Sloth going second · Thrust toolbox
PreserveGarura / Mudragon · Prayers timing · Murakumo follow-up
Graveyard engine

Trade one turn for their grave.

Different Dimension Ground is strongest before the opponent sends or Tributes key resources. Thrust improves access after they interact.

Bring inDifferent Dimension Ground · 2 Thrust · Fuwalos as appropriate
WatchYour Mirror recursion · Fallen sends · Lamia / Magnamhut line
Backrow / control

Reduce dead monster interaction.

Duster and Thrust pressure set cards. Do not overcommit Ritual resources into a board wipe when one recursive boss is enough.

Bring inHarpie’s Feather Duster · 2 Thrust · Magatama where relevant
Common cutsFuwalos · Magnamhut without targets · Impulse where weak

Going first

Layer searchable interaction.

  • Songs of the DominatorsSet it; avoid the hand lock.
  • Different Dimension GroundStrong into GY-dependent starters.
  • Second WousuFor boss-heavy boards that demand repeated removal.

Going second

Convert board size into selection.

  • Pot of SlothFind the best breaker and bottom unwanted engine pieces.
  • Forbidden DropletCompress negates and put useful cards in the GY.
  • Triple Tactics ThrustTurns their interaction into your best Normal Spell or Trap.

Conditional cuts

Cut cards because their role is absent.

  • MagnamhutWhen LIGHT/DARK targets are scarce.
  • Great PurificationOften the first cut going second.
  • Super PolymerizationTrim only when legal pairs are genuinely sparse.

Do not auto-side

Do not remove Synchros while retaining full Fydraulis mode. Do not hand-activate Songs by habit. Do not treat Pot of Sloth as unlimited raw draw.

Core doctrine

Preserve a reliable engine count after siding. Replace interaction with interaction instead of cutting the Ritual core indiscriminately.
