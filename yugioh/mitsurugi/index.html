01 · Deck identity

Value is hidden in the Tribute.

The build does not chase the largest possible board. It establishes a Ritual boss, banks reactive cards, and makes the opponent trade into Tribute effects that replace themselves.

儀

Exact-level Ritual engine

Mitsurugi Ritual turns two Level 4 Reptiles in the Deck into a Level 8 boss plus multiple Tribute triggers.

鎖

Interaction in different zones

Impulse, Fydraulis, Super Polymerization, Fallen and set Mitsurugi cards attack from distinct angles.

還

Recursive bosses

Murakumo and Futsu convert interruption into new cards, revival and another wave of pressure.

03 · Turn plan

Choose the posture before the line.

Going first, this is layered Ritual control. Going second, it lets the opponent expose targets before converting them into tempo.

04 · Line library

Repeatable lines, not fantasy hands.

Compact templates designed to branch with the opponent’s interaction.

Ritual + Level 8 boss → two Level 4 triggers
  1. Activate Mitsurugi Ritual to Ritual Summon the Level 8 Reptile from hand.
  2. Use Aramasa + Saji from the Deck when both are available.
  3. Build the Tribute-trigger Chain so the most important search is insulated.
  4. Going first, favor Great Purification or Prayers; going second, favor immediate removal and extension.
Habakiri access → Saji conversion
  1. Reveal Habakiri and summon Saji from the Deck.
  2. Tribute Saji to search the missing Mitsurugi Spell/Trap.
  3. Use Ritual or Prayers to convert the hand into a properly summoned boss and preserve the revival loop.
  4. Do not burn Habakiri’s once-per-Duel hand effect on a line that does not improve the final interaction map.
Lamia → Magnamhut protection line
  1. Use Lamia as Habakiri access or Ritual material, placing it in the GY.
  2. On either turn, Special Summon Bystial Magnamhut by banishing Lamia when a legal LIGHT/DARK target is available.
  3. Lamia’s banish trigger protects a Reptile boss from effect destruction through the end of the next turn.
  4. Treat this as free upside, not a line worth weakening the deck to force.
Fydraulis full reveal → Synchro send
  1. Keep all five Synchros in the Extra Deck before the first full Fydraulis resolution.
  2. Reveal the required Synchro bank, Special Summon Fydraulis and send the Synchro whose GY effect best answers the board.
  3. Malong is the bounce; Packbit preserves WATER functionality after Impulse; other sends support the needed follow-up.
  4. Resolve activated non-Synchro Extra Deck effects first because Fydraulis creates a lingering restriction.
The Fallen & The Virtuous send map
  1. Use Fallen to remove a relevant face-up card without committing your Normal Summon.
  2. Send Albion when End Phase access to another Fallen matters.
  3. Send The Dragon that Devours the Dogma for direct follow-up; send Ecclesia and the Dark Dragon when its graveyard state matters.
  4. Resolve planned Extra Deck summons before Dogmatika Ecclesia searches.
Dyna Mondo recovery pivot
  1. Use a Ritual Monster plus another body to make Dyna Mondo after the engine has already replaced those cards.
  2. Its on-summon interaction can reset a field card and a Ritual in the GY.
  3. On the opponent’s turn, Tribute Dyna Mondo to add or Special Summon a properly summoned Ritual Monster.
  4. Reviving Murakumo turns the pivot into a board wipe and another search.

Common conditional cuts

Magnamhut without LIGHT/DARK targets; Great Purification going second; Talent when Main Phase monster effects are unlikely; 1–2 Super Polymerization when legal pairs are sparse.

Do not auto-side

Do not remove Synchros if you still expect full Fydraulis mode. Do not hand-activate Songs by habit. Do not treat Sloth as unlimited raw draw.

Core doctrine

Preserve twelve-to-fifteen reliable engine cards after siding. Replace interaction with interaction rather than cutting the Ritual core indiscriminately.
